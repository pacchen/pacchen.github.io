About Me.

I’m Paul (Yi-Chia) Chen. I grew up in Taiwan and came to the United States for college. At the University of Southern California, I earned my B.S. in Electrical and Computer Engineering and M.S. in Computer Engineering, and I’m now pursuing my Ph.D.

I work with Prof. Christopher Torng in the ACORN Research Group. My research focuses on compiler-directed optimization for energy-efficient machine learning accelerators and silicon prototyping. I enjoy working across the stack, from the decisions a compiler makes to the hardware that executes them.

Before joining ACORN, I researched GNN inference acceleration in Prof. Viktor Prasanna’s FPGA/Parallel Computing Lab. I also spent a year at EMD Electronics working on machine learning and data pipelines for semiconductor manufacturing.

Beyond my own research, I’ve mentored students through chip design and tapeout as a teaching assistant. In 2025, I completed the regional I-Corps West Hub ZAP and BOOM programs.
